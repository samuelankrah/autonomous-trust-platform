The payments report request

A program asks to read a protected report. The system checks who it is, what it is allowed to do, and whether its proof is fresh. Then it decides, and the decision is enforced.

Explore: the story in plain words

Label: Conceptual explanation

Think of a car arriving at a workshop:

  • The workload is the driver, arriving with a job to do.
  • The identity is the driver proving who they are.
  • The grant is the manager's written permission, naming what this driver may do.
  • The check-up report (attestation evidence) is the car's own report about itself, with a time on it: what it is running and whether it has been messed with.
  • The decision point is the service desk. It checks all three together and issues a signed work order.
  • The enforcement point is the gate to the bay. It checks the work order before letting the car through.
  • The protected resource is the bay, and the report inside it.
  • The evidence ledger is the workshop logbook, where every check and outcome is written down in order.
Where the car story stops working

The story is a memory aid only, and it has limits. It does not capture signing, binding (a decision tied to one request), or checking whether permission was taken back.

In the real model, the grant is more than a note from a manager: it carries a bounded permission from an authority, for this report and this action only. And the gate does more than glance at the work order: it checks a signed decision that is protected against changes and tied to this one request.

          Repair and reset

          Diagnosis

          What broke, which stage caught it, and what will you change to repair it? All three answers are required before repair controls unlock.

          Repair

          Repair never broadens authority. A repair restores the happy path permissions exactly, it never adds a new permission, a wider resource, or a different action.

          For replay, the rerun is the normal path for a fresh second request: a fresh decision is obtained for the new request through the normal decision step and enforced for that request. The original decision stays bound to its own request, and the replay rejection remains recorded as evidence.
