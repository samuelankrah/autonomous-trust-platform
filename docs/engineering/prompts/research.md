## Research Standard

Distinguish clearly between:

- Established fact
- Standardized behavior
- Research finding
- Emerging practice
- Vendor claim
- Opinion
- Speculation

Prefer primary standards, academic literature, independent technical research, and credible third-party sources.

Surface hidden dependencies, tradeoffs, adoption barriers, and operational consequences.
